# More Latin-script languages in the English check

## Question
The English check tells Latin-script languages apart from English by their function words, listed
for Spanish, German, French, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch and Polish. Which further languages
(Turkish,
Czech, Vietnamese, Romanian, Swedish…) should be added, and should the list follow the operator
locales fraim actually ships rather than grow ahead of them?

## Why it matters
A language outside the list passes the check: an operator speaking it would see the foundation drift
into their language with nothing to stop it. Non-Latin scripts are already caught whatever the
language.
